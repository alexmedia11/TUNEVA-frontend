What is TUNEVA?

TUNEVA is a digital platform featuring Tap Tap activities, Language Earn voice lessons and Listen & Earn content experiences. The public website introduces these features and explains the available membership options.

TUNEVA

Explore the platform

Tap Tap presents coin-tapping activities and tasks made available to a member's level. Language Earn focuses on short voice lessons and sharing greetings and expressions in languages such as Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin and Efik. Listen & Earn features videos, music and voice notes.

Membership and participation

The public site describes Pro and Max membership options. Review the displayed activation fees, minimum withdrawal thresholds and applicable terms carefully. Promotional figures are not guaranteed income; rewards depend on eligible tasks, completion, approval and platform terms.
