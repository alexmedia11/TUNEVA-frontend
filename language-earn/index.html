Voice-based mini lessons

The TUNEVA promotional guide describes short voice lessons built around greetings and everyday expressions. It describes voice-based participation without requiring a camera for this activity.

TUNEVA

Languages and expressions

The public TUNEVA materials mention Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, Efik and more. The feature is designed around sharing language and voice through simple expressions.

TUNEVA

Participation and rewards

Follow the instructions for each available lesson. The public plan comparison displays promotional Language Earn figures, but rewards are not guaranteed and depend on eligibility, completion, approval and platform terms.
