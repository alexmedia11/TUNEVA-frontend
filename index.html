BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your questions.
Made clearer.

What happens when I sign up?

You provide your registration details and selected plan. Follow the platform's registration and payment instructions to continue.

How is payment confirmed?

Complete payment through the available Squad checkout instructions. Your payment must be successfully verified before activation can proceed.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Earnings are not guaranteed. Rewards depend on the availability of eligible tasks, successful completion, approval and the platform's applicable terms.

Do I need a camera for Language Earn?

The promotional guide describes voice-based mini lessons and does not require a camera for that activity.

Can I withdraw the welcome cashback immediately?

Cashback withdrawal eligibility and timing depend on the applicable promotion terms. Confirm the conditions before making a payment.

Where do my registration details go?

Your details are submitted through the TUNEVA registration process for account setup and activation. Review the platform's privacy information before submitting personal information.
